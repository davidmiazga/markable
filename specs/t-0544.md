# Center dock drop-zone indicator labels and align drop zones to the workspace container

- Task: `t-0544`
- Bead: `markable-ubn`
- Status: `briefing`

## Problem
When dragging a panel header across dock edges, the 4 dock drop-zone overlays (`.panel-dock-drop-zone--left`, `--right`, `--top`, `--bottom`) do not reliably center their label text (`Left Dock`, `Right Dock`, `Top Dock`, `Bottom Dock`). In particular, the left and right zones are only `56px` wide without `text-align: center`, causing two-word labels to wrap left-aligned inside a narrow vertical bar, and the drop-zone overlay container uses fixed viewport coordinates rather than aligning to the `#app-row` workspace bounds.

## In scope
- Update `.panel-dock-drop-zones` and `.panel-dock-drop-zone` styling in `src/sidebar/sidebar.css` and DOM creation in `src/sidebar/panel-reorder-drag.ts` so every drop-zone label is horizontally and vertically centered.
- Position the `.panel-dock-drop-zones` overlay relative to `#app-row` bounds (falling back to viewport bounds when `#app-row` has zero dimensions in headless tests) so top, bottom, left, and right drop zones are symmetrically aligned within the workspace area.
- Wrap drop-zone text in a centered label span (`.panel-dock-drop-zone-label`) with `text-align: center`, `display: flex`, `align-items: center`, `justify-content: center`, and clean single-line or balanced centered layout so text is never off-center in any of the 4 drop zones.

## Out of scope
- Changing dock side names or adding new dock positions beyond Left, Right, Top, and Bottom.
- Modifying panel lock or Workspace Panel-Locations preset persistence.

## UX / behavior
- While dragging a panel header, all four dock drop zones (`Left Dock`, `Right Dock`, `Top Dock`, `Bottom Dock`) display their text centered both horizontally and vertically inside the dashed drop-zone box.
- Left and right drop zones are wide enough (or styled with centered flex/text layout) and symmetrically inset within `#app-row` so the label text sits dead-center vertically and horizontally.
- Top and bottom drop zones are symmetrically inset within `#app-row` and center their label text both vertically and horizontally.

## Tests
- Unit tests in `tests/sidebar-manager.test.ts` verifying `.panel-dock-drop-zone` elements and `.panel-dock-drop-zone-label` children are created for all 4 dock sides with centered styling and that cross-dock drag-and-drop continues to pass.
